## Original visual design {#original-assets}

The screens and workflows of the desktop interface can be inspired by those of other workstations.
A provenance record states that an icon, image, texture or font is original, or that it is
redistributable under a recorded license. A reference capture is a screenshot or a recording of
another product, taken for a private comparison of workflow and spacing. The reference images of
the product are images of its own views, which its visual conformance cases compare with.

### EU-0018-073 {#EU-0018-073}

The product MUST use an original identity, palette, icons, graphics and artwork.

### EU-0018-074 {#EU-0018-074}

The product MUST NOT copy another product's assets, icons or exact combination of layout, color, typography and artwork.

### EU-0018-075 {#EU-0018-075}

Each icon, image, texture and font in a release MUST have a provenance record that shows it is original or redistributable under a recorded license.

### EU-0018-076 {#EU-0018-076}

Each interface framework dependency of a release MUST have a passing license check.

### EU-0018-077 {#EU-0018-077}

A release, the repository and the public reference images MUST NOT hold a reference capture of another product or an asset extracted from one.

### EU-0018-078 {#EU-0018-078}

The release check MUST refuse a build whose provenance or license records are missing, failed or don't match the exact build.

### EU-0018-079 {#EU-0018-079}

The product MUST NOT claim affiliation with the maker of another product.

### EU-0018-080 {#EU-0018-080}

Visual conformance cases MUST compare views with the product's own reference images at a declared scale, font and renderer.
