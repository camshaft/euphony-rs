## Speech during a session {#voice}

Participants talk through an ordinary call alongside the session or through voice built into the
session, and the call stays the fallback whenever the session's voice path is unavailable.
Headphones are the supported setup with both. Voice built into the session travels on the session's
connection as a stream separate from the program audio, and its echo canceller takes what each
participant's output plays as its reference. The session offers push-to-talk and an open microphone,
and can lower the program audio while someone speaks, as the talkback of a studio does.

### EU-0013-200 {#EU-0013-200}

Participants MUST be able to talk during a session while they listen to the mix through the session.

### EU-0013-201 {#EU-0013-201}

Speech MUST NOT enter any recording, export, project bus or mix.

### EU-0013-202 {#EU-0013-202}

With headphones, program audio MUST NOT reach the other participants through the microphone of a participant.

### EU-0013-203 {#EU-0013-203}

With an external call, the session MUST ask each participant whether they listen on headphones.

### EU-0013-204 {#EU-0013-204}

With an external call, the session MUST show every participant a warning while any participant listens on speakers.

### EU-0013-205 {#EU-0013-205}

The voice built into the session MUST cancel echo against the program audio and the voices that the output of each participant plays.

### EU-0013-206 {#EU-0013-206}

The session MUST measure the program audio left in each outgoing voice stream against its reference.

### EU-0013-207 {#EU-0013-207}

Where the session cannot confirm that program audio returning through the microphone of a participant is suppressed, it MUST warn every participant.

### EU-0013-208 {#EU-0013-208}

Where suppression for a participant is not confirmed, the session MUST offer that participant push-to-talk.

### EU-0013-209 {#EU-0013-209}

A participant MUST be able to choose whether their voice plays with low delay or delayed to the aligned presentation delay.

### EU-0013-226 {#EU-0013-226}

The voice built into the session MUST offer each participant both push-to-talk and an open microphone.

### EU-0013-210 {#EU-0013-210}

The session MUST show the microphone state of each participant to everyone.

### EU-0013-211 {#EU-0013-211}

A muted participant MUST NOT send audio.

### EU-0013-212 {#EU-0013-212}

Capturing a microphone for voice MUST need the consent of the participant.

### EU-0013-213 {#EU-0013-213}

The session MAY lower the program audio while someone speaks.
