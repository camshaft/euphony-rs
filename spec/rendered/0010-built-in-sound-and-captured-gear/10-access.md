## Access, sharing and rights {#access}

Captures, instrument maps, dumps and models stay on the owner's paired computers. Sharing a model
with a collaborator needs the owner's approval and a statement of the rights claimed, and approval
to record doesn't authorize sharing. Owning a device doesn't establish a right to copy or publish
the sound assets it holds, and euphony doesn't decide what is lawful: a qualified legal review does.
A model is named by the musician, without the branding of a manufacturer. The identity of a model
establishes which object it is, not that it can be trusted or that its use is permitted. Every
capture, cancellation, fit, report and attachment is a typed command, available alike to the
interface and to assistants, and a browser companion controls the computer the gear is wired to
rather than capturing on its own.

### EU-0010-192 {#EU-0010-192}

Captures, instrument maps, dumps and models MUST stay on the owner's paired computers unless the owner shares them.

### EU-0010-193 {#EU-0010-193}

euphony MUST refuse a capture or a model to a computer that is not paired with its owner.

### EU-0010-194 {#EU-0010-194}

Sharing a model with a collaborator MUST need the owner's approval and a statement of the rights claimed.

### EU-0010-195 {#EU-0010-195}

The approval of a capture session MUST NOT authorize sharing what it records.

### EU-0010-196 {#EU-0010-196}

euphony MUST NOT name a model with the product name or trademark of a manufacturer.

### EU-0010-197 {#EU-0010-197}

A model MUST keep the provenance and the restrictions of the captures it was derived from.

### EU-0010-198 {#EU-0010-198}

An imported instrument map MUST NOT authorize a persistent write to a device.

### EU-0010-199 {#EU-0010-199}

A change of device identity or of map digest MUST need revalidation before a capture emits again.

### EU-0010-200 {#EU-0010-200}

Each capture, cancellation, fit, report and attachment of a model MUST be a typed command available alike to the interface and to assistants.

### EU-0010-201 {#EU-0010-201}

A browser companion MUST NOT capture from a device or run model inference on its own.
