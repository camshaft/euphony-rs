## Listening results as evidence {#listening}

A listening result from a listening session keeps the listener, the project revision, the sink the
listener chose, the codec and quality the listener received, the level alignment, the output profile
and the reported timing uncertainty. A comment link lets its holder comment and nothing else.
Talkback stays outside capture, and this RFC doesn't add voice transcription.

### EU-0019-180 {#EU-0019-180}

A listening result MUST keep the actual listener's identity, the project revision, the selected sink, the received codec and quality, the level alignment, the output profile and the reported timing uncertainty.

### EU-0019-181 {#EU-0019-181}

A listening result MUST record the quality the listener reported receiving, not the quality the host intended.

### EU-0019-182 {#EU-0019-182}

A comment link MUST NOT allow anything beyond comments.

### EU-0019-183 {#EU-0019-183}

A listener MUST choose whether their contribution is used as evidence about their taste.

### EU-0019-184 {#EU-0019-184}

A listener's statement MUST NOT become another person's approved preference.

### EU-0019-185 {#EU-0019-185}

A listening result of mixed quality or without level alignment MUST NOT establish a calibrated preference about sound.

### EU-0019-186 {#EU-0019-186}

Euphony MUST NOT capture talkback as learning evidence.
