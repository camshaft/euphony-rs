## Measured effects of a mechanism {#effects}

Each adopted mechanism is measured before and after its activation on a comparable workflow,
project size and device profile: its corrections, undo and revert outcomes, explicit usefulness
ratings and active task time. Time spent waiting or listening is kept apart from editing time. The
effect report records the event definitions, the eligible counts, the observation coverage, the
mechanism and runtime versions and any concurrent changes, and shows its uncertainty and the
owner's own assessment. The trial the owner approved states its acceptance predicates; euphony
doesn't set a product-wide threshold of its own. A mechanism is retired through a proposal that
explains the result and the owner's acceptance of it, and its history stays.

### EU-0019-140 {#EU-0019-140}

Euphony MUST measure each adopted mechanism's corrections, undo and revert outcomes, usefulness ratings and active task time before and after its activation, on a comparable workflow, project size and device profile.

### EU-0019-141 {#EU-0019-141}

The measurement MUST keep time spent waiting or listening apart from editing time.

### EU-0019-142 {#EU-0019-142}

An effect report MUST record its event definitions, eligible counts, observation coverage, mechanism and runtime versions and concurrent changes.

### EU-0019-143 {#EU-0019-143}

An effect report MUST show its uncertainty and the owner's assessment.

### EU-0019-144 {#EU-0019-144}

An effect report MUST NOT claim from an uncontrolled comparison that a mechanism caused a change.

### EU-0019-145 {#EU-0019-145}

A shorter session or fewer undos alone MUST NOT count as evidence of better music.

### EU-0019-146 {#EU-0019-146}

Each trial MUST state the acceptance predicates the owner accepted with it.

### EU-0019-147 {#EU-0019-147}

Where an observed report fails its trial's acceptance predicates or shows a safety failure, euphony MUST suspend the mechanism and restore its prior safe configuration.

### EU-0019-148 {#EU-0019-148}

Where the evidence is incomplete, an effect report MUST mark the benefit as unverified.

### EU-0019-149 {#EU-0019-149}

An effect report MUST NOT report missing efficacy evidence as a success.

### EU-0019-150 {#EU-0019-150}

Retiring a mechanism MUST be the owner's acceptance of a retirement proposal that explains the result.

### EU-0019-151 {#EU-0019-151}

A retired mechanism version MUST keep its history.

### EU-0019-152 {#EU-0019-152}

The owner MUST be able to disable a mechanism at any time, with immediate effect.

### EU-0019-153 {#EU-0019-153}

A replacement mechanism MUST be tested on its own before the owner accepts it.
