## Character and original sound {#character}

The character of a strip sets how its equalizer, compressor and color stages sound: the shapes of
the equalizer's curves, the compressor's detector and gain element, and the color stage's
saturation. A project has one character, as a studio has one console, and a track can use another.
A character is a versioned set of processor versions and their profile data. Characters are
original, written from public signal-processing literature, or come from gear the musician owns and
captures, as RFC 0010 specifies capture. The strip doesn't copy a named console's circuits, panels or
name.

### EU-0011-158 {#EU-0011-158}

Each project MUST have one character, which sets how the equalizer, compressor and color stages of its strips sound.

### EU-0011-159 {#EU-0011-159}

A track MAY use a character other than its project's.

### EU-0011-160 {#EU-0011-160}

A character MUST be a versioned set of processor versions and their profile data.

### EU-0011-161 {#EU-0011-161}

A character, a preset or the text of the interface MUST NOT carry the name of a console maker or of its products without recorded legal clearance.

### EU-0011-162 {#EU-0011-162}

A stage MUST NOT copy the circuits or the panel design of a named console.

### EU-0011-163 {#EU-0011-163}

A built-in character or preset MUST NOT include a model fitted to a commercial unit without recorded legal clearance.

### EU-0011-164 {#EU-0011-164}

A captured model used as a color stage MUST NOT leave the musician's computers without the musician's approval.
