## Simple and full controls {#controls}

Each stage shows a simple view of one to three controls, each named for what it does to the sound,
and its full controls one action away. Each simple control is a curve over the stage's parameters,
centered on the starting point, with the range the role profile sets, and moving a full control moves
that center, so both views always show the same settings. Simple controls are parameters like any
other, so automation and modulation reach them, as RFC 0012 specifies modulation. The phone
controllers and control surfaces of RFC 0017 show the simple view.

### EU-0011-113 {#EU-0011-113}

Each stage MUST offer a simple view of fewer controls than its full set.

### EU-0011-114 {#EU-0011-114}

Each simple control MUST be named for its effect on the sound.

### EU-0011-115 {#EU-0011-115}

The musician MUST be able to open a stage's full controls from its simple view with one action.

### EU-0011-116 {#EU-0011-116}

The simple view and the full view of a stage MUST always show the same settings.

### EU-0011-117 {#EU-0011-117}

The simple view MUST hold low cut and high cut for the filters, amount for the gate, amount and tone for the equalizer, amount and speed for the compressor, amount for the de-esser, drive and tone for the color stage, and an amount for each send.

### EU-0011-118 {#EU-0011-118}

The equalizer's amount MUST scale its dialed corrections from none to twice their starting size.

### EU-0011-119 {#EU-0011-119}

The equalizer's tone MUST tilt its response from darker to brighter.

### EU-0011-120 {#EU-0011-120}

The compressor's amount MUST move its threshold and its level-matched output gain together.

### EU-0011-121 {#EU-0011-121}

The compressor's speed MUST move its attack and its release together.

### EU-0011-122 {#EU-0011-122}

The center of each simple control MUST be the stage's starting point with the changes of its full controls applied.

### EU-0011-123 {#EU-0011-123}

The range of each simple control MUST be the one the role profile sets.

### EU-0011-124 {#EU-0011-124}

The full view MUST show every parameter of the stage.

### EU-0011-125 {#EU-0011-125}

The full view MUST show the stage's analysis display, such as its gain reduction or its spectrum.

### EU-0011-126 {#EU-0011-126}

Each simple control MUST be a target of automation and of modulation, as any parameter is.

### EU-0011-127 {#EU-0011-127}

Every client MUST draw both views of a stage from the same description of its controls.

### EU-0011-128 {#EU-0011-128}

The phone controllers and the control surfaces MUST show the simple view of each stage.
