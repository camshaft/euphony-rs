## Listening and acceptance {#acceptance}

The blind comparison plays the original passage and the candidate's render of the same notes, at
matched levels and in random order, and records the musician's result. Failing to tell the two
apart isn't proof that they are the same. Acceptance adds a candidate to the project as an
instrument track or an effect chain, as an ordinary revision that the musician previews and undoes
in one step. Before acceptance, a rebuild leaves the project as it was.

### EU-0023-037 {#EU-0023-037}

The blind comparison of a candidate MUST play the original passage and the candidate's render of the same notes at matched levels and in random order.

### EU-0023-038 {#EU-0023-038}

The blind comparison MUST record the musician's result with its candidate.

### EU-0023-039 {#EU-0023-039}

A blind result in which the musician could not tell the two apart MUST NOT be reported as proof that they are the same.

### EU-0023-040 {#EU-0023-040}

Acceptance MUST add the candidate to the project as an instrument track or an effect chain in one revision that the musician can preview.

### EU-0023-041 {#EU-0023-041}

The musician MUST be able to undo an acceptance in one step.

### EU-0023-042 {#EU-0023-042}

A rebuild MUST NOT change the project before one of its candidates is accepted.

### EU-0023-043 {#EU-0023-043}

Acceptance MUST refuse a candidate that has no recorded blind result of the musician's own, whoever asks for it.

### EU-0023-044 {#EU-0023-044}

An accepted sound MUST stay linked to the records of the candidate it came from.
