## Measuring and offering candidates {#measurement}

The comparison aligns the rebuilt render and the original by their onsets and matches their levels,
then measures their difference in spectra at several resolutions, in the spectral envelope, in the
attack and decay of each note, in pitch in cents and in the noise between notes. Some notes of the
passage are held back from fitting where the passage holds enough of them, and the final
measurement reads them. A candidate is a rebuilt sound that a rebuild offers the musician, with its
measurements. Different structures can measure equally close and sound different, which only the
musician's listening decides, so a rebuild offers several candidates.

### EU-0023-026 {#EU-0023-026}

The comparison MUST align the rebuilt render and the original by their onsets before it measures them.

### EU-0023-027 {#EU-0023-027}

The comparison MUST match the levels of the rebuilt render and the original before it measures them.

### EU-0023-028 {#EU-0023-028}

The comparison MUST measure the difference in spectra at several resolutions, in the spectral envelope, in the attack and decay of each note, in pitch in cents and in the noise between notes.

### EU-0023-029 {#EU-0023-029}

Each measure MUST show its value together with its interval.

### EU-0023-030 {#EU-0023-030}

The comparison MUST NOT present its measures as a single perceptual score.

### EU-0023-031 {#EU-0023-031}

Where the passage holds enough notes, the final measurement MUST use notes that the fit didn't read.

### EU-0023-032 {#EU-0023-032}

Where the passage holds too few notes to hold some back, the final measurement MUST say that it measured the fitted notes alone.

### EU-0023-033 {#EU-0023-033}

A rebuild MUST offer the musician the several candidates that measure closest to the original.

### EU-0023-034 {#EU-0023-034}

Each candidate MUST be an immutable record of its structure, its settings, each measure for the whole passage and for each note, its held-back result, its measured processing cost and its declared added delay.

### EU-0023-035 {#EU-0023-035}

A rebuilt sound MUST NOT be called a match of its original before its measurements and the musician's blind listening result exist.

### EU-0023-036 {#EU-0023-036}

Each candidate offered MUST have a recorded comparison with its original passage.
