## Records, repeatability and rights {#records}

The record of a rebuild holds the source digest and interval, the analysis results it read, the
versions of its planner, its fitter, its measurements and its processors, its seeds, its held-back
notes, and each structure proposed for it, whether the planner or an assistant proposed it. A
repeat from the same record fits the same structures again. A fit computes its sums in a fixed
order on one numerical profile. An accepted sound is saved in the project as processors and
settings, and its source passage stays in the owner's store.

### EU-0023-049 {#EU-0023-049}

The record of a rebuild MUST hold its source digest and interval, the analysis results it read, the versions of its planner, fitter, measurements and processors, its seeds, its held-back notes and each structure proposed for it.

### EU-0023-050 {#EU-0023-050}

A repeat of a rebuild from its record MUST fit the recorded structures again without asking for new proposals.

### EU-0023-051 {#EU-0023-051}

A repeat of a rebuild from the same record MUST offer the same candidates with the same measurements.

### EU-0023-052 {#EU-0023-052}

Each candidate MUST be addressed by the digest of its content.

### EU-0023-053 {#EU-0023-053}

A fit computed on another numerical profile MUST have another identity.

### EU-0023-054 {#EU-0023-054}

An accepted sound MUST be saved in the project as processors and settings without the source audio.

### EU-0023-055 {#EU-0023-055}

An accepted sound MUST play without the recording, the plug-in or the gear it was rebuilt from.

### EU-0023-056 {#EU-0023-056}

A project shared with a collaborator MUST NOT hold the source passage of a rebuilt sound unless the musician shares the source too.

### EU-0023-057 {#EU-0023-057}

Rebuilding MUST NOT inspect the code of a plug-in or extract its presets.
