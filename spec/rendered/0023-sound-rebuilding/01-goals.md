## Goals and end state {#goals}

Rebuilding a sound takes a passage of a recording or a sample, or the captures of an instrument or
an effect the musician owns, and offers rebuilt sounds made of euphony's built-in instruments and
effects. A rebuilt sound is a structure of built-in processors with their settings, which the
musician plays with new notes and adjusts with ordinary controls. Each rebuilt sound is measured
against its original and listened to blind by the musician before the musician accepts it. The notes
that a rebuild plays come from the musician or from the analysis of the passage; rebuilding rebuilds
the instrument and its effects, not the performance. Rebuilding builds a chain of its own that
measures close to the original, and doesn't identify the product, preset or chain that made it.
The analysis of a passage, the built-in processors, the capture of gear and its blind comparison,
the workers that render, the content store and the trust model are each specified in the RFC of
their own component, and this RFC uses them.

### EU-0023-001 {#EU-0023-001}

A musician MUST be able to rebuild a sound from a chosen passage of a recording or a sample.

### EU-0023-002 {#EU-0023-002}

A musician MUST be able to rebuild the sound of an instrument or an effect they own from its captures.

### EU-0023-003 {#EU-0023-003}

A rebuilt sound MUST be made only of built-in instruments and effects with their settings.

### EU-0023-004 {#EU-0023-004}

A rebuilt sound MUST play the new notes that the musician plays on it.

### EU-0023-005 {#EU-0023-005}

Each setting of a rebuilt sound MUST be an ordinary control of a built-in instrument or effect that the musician can adjust.

### EU-0023-006 {#EU-0023-006}

Rebuilding MUST NOT name the product, preset or chain that made the original sound.

### EU-0023-007 {#EU-0023-007}

A rebuilt sound MUST NOT carry the name of its source or of a commercial product.

### EU-0023-008 {#EU-0023-008}

A rebuilt sound MUST NOT hold the performance of its original.

### EU-0023-009 {#EU-0023-009}

Rebuilt sounds MUST NOT be published or distributed as presets.
