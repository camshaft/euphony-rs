## Rebuilding owned gear {#owned-gear}

For an effect, capture records the gear's input and its output; for a synthesizer, capture plays
notes and controls and records its sound. A rebuild from gear reads those captures by their
identity and fits built-in processors to them. The learned and hybrid models that capture makes of
the gear stay results of capture, beside the rebuilt sound, and the musician compares the three. A
rebuilt synthesizer voice maps the synthesizer's controls to built-in controls where it can.

### EU-0023-045 {#EU-0023-045}

A rebuild from gear MUST fit built-in processors alone to the captures of the gear, without learned parts.

### EU-0023-046 {#EU-0023-046}

A rebuild from gear MUST compare its result with the models that capture made of the same gear, by the same measures and the same blind comparison.

### EU-0023-047 {#EU-0023-047}

A rebuilt synthesizer voice MUST map each measured control of the synthesizer to a built-in control where its structure holds one.

### EU-0023-048 {#EU-0023-048}

A rebuilt synthesizer voice MUST list each measured control of the synthesizer that it doesn't reproduce.
